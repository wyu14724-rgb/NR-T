# Data and results licence

The files under `results/` (CSV, JSON, TXT and NPZ) are released under the
Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

This covers the derived artefacts produced by the authors: table files, result
tables, score dumps and statistical outputs. It does **not** cover the raw
datasets (ToN-IoT, UNSW-NB15, CIC-IDS2017), which are redistributed by their
original providers under their own terms, or the frozen model checkpoints,
which are not redistributed at all.

If you use these files, please cite the manuscript (see `CITATION.cff`).
